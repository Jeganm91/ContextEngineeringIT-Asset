# Vendor Licensing Audit — Call Notes (Design Suite Pro Renewal)

Attendees: IT Asset Management Team, Procurement Lead, Design Suite Pro vendor
account manager.

The call opened with a general review of the account history. Design Suite Pro has
been under an enterprise agreement with the organization since 2022, originally
provisioned for the creative team of twelve seats. Usage has grown steadily each
year as the design function expanded into marketing and product documentation.
The vendor account manager walked through the current tier structure, noting that
enterprise agreements above fifty seats qualify for a volume discount that scales
in three bands, and that the organization is currently in the second band based on
last quarter's seat count.

There was a lengthy discussion about historical invoicing discrepancies from two
years ago, which were resolved at the time through a one-time credit and are not
relevant to the current renewal. The team also reviewed the standard support SLA,
which includes a four-hour response window for priority tickets and a dedicated
technical account manager for accounts over one hundred seats.

The vendor account manager then described upcoming changes to the platform's
collaboration features, expected to ship in the next two release cycles, and how
those features might affect per-seat pricing in future renewal cycles. This
generated some discussion about whether the organization should consolidate seats
across departments before the next enterprise agreement renewal to maximize the
volume discount band, though no decision was reached on that point during the call.

Several minutes were spent recapping the organization's internal software request
process for context, since the vendor account manager was unfamiliar with how
requests are routed internally before reaching Procurement.

Near the end of the call, the vendor account manager flagged the one item requiring
action before the renewal can be finalized: the current enterprise agreement
auto-renews in 30 days unless the organization submits a formal seat-count
adjustment request beforehand, and failing to do so before the renewal date will
lock in the current (lower) discount band for the full following year regardless
of actual seat growth. The Procurement Lead confirmed this action item would be
picked up separately and the call was closed.

# Premium Print Plan — Service Tier Overview

When a team member has been independently covering the recurring cost of a
Premium Print Plan subscription before it becomes a shared office requirement,
that ongoing charge can be recovered through Expense Management once the
printer is listed in the approved hardware catalog.

This route sits outside the standard procurement chain and does not need
Procurement Lead sign-off, since no new corporate purchase is involved.

This plan concerns printer hardware service levels only. It does not apply to
software licensing, software cost recovery, or any subscription-based
application spend; those are governed by the IT Asset Management team's
separate license and premium-software approval policies.

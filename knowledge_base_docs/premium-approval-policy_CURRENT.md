# Premium Software Approval Policy

Premium software licenses require Procurement Lead approval in addition to
manager approval. Procurement reviews cost, seat count and budget code.

Policy fact: renewal_period_months = 12. License utilization is reviewed for
renewal every 12 months unless flagged for early review by Procurement.

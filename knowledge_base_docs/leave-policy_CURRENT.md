# Annual Leave and Time-Off Policy

Employees accrue 1.5 days of paid leave per month, credited on the first of
each month. Leave requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 3
working days in advance and require manager approval.

Unused leave up to 10 days may be carried forward into the next calendar
year; any balance above that is forfeited.

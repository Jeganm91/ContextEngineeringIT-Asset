# Individually-Held Software Costs — Recovery Path

When a team member has been independently covering the recurring cost of a
tool before it becomes a work requirement, that ongoing charge can be
recovered through Expense Management once the tool is listed in the approved
catalog.

This route sits outside the standard licensing chain and does not need
Procurement Lead sign-off, since no new corporate purchase is involved.
